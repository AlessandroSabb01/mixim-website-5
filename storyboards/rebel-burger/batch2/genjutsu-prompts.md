# Rebel Burger — Prompts FX pour les hooks (Genjutsu / Kling / image-to-video)

**Comment les utiliser**
- **Image de départ** = la frame de ta vraie vidéo juste avant l'effet (caméra fixe, clean plate tourné).
- Durée : 3–5 s. Format 9:16.
- Garde le même prompt « négatif » pour tous.
- Les prompts sont en anglais : les modèles vidéo les comprennent mieux.

**Négatif (tous les hooks)**
`no text, no subtitles, no watermark, no extra people, no face change, no extra fingers, no camera shake, no cuts, keep the man's face, cap, apron and RB logo identical`

---

## 1 — Burger Day : explosion en cendres
**Start frame :** Olivier tient le burger fast-food écrasé dans sa main.
```
Locked-off medium shot in a dark smash burger kitchen with red lights. The man squeezes the squashed fast-food burger in his fist; on impact it bursts into a cloud of black ash and glowing orange embers that freeze in mid-air for a beat, then drift slowly upward. Through the smoke his other hand rises holding a fresh raw ball of ground beef, he grins at camera. Realistic particles, warm rim light, 24fps, 4 seconds.
```

## 2 — Smash Badass : arrêt du temps + lévitation
**Start frame :** plan par-dessus l'épaule, Olivier baisse ses lunettes, burger sur le comptoir.
```
Slow push-in over the man's shoulder toward the smash burger on the steel counter. As he lowers his sunglasses, time freezes: smoke and grease droplets stop in mid-air, the burger slowly lifts off the counter and rotates 360 degrees in a soft golden glow, cheese curds glistening. Then time resumes and his tattooed hand snatches the floating burger out of the air. Cinematic, photorealistic, 4 seconds.
```

## 3 — Primeau : poutine-fusée
**Start frame :** grand-angle au ras du comptoir, Olivier pousse la poutine.
```
Low wide-angle shot at counter level. The man shoves the loaded poutine box across the steel counter toward the camera; it accelerates like a rocket, leaving a blazing fire trail and sparks, motion blur on the counter, then stops abruptly 5 cm from the lens, steam rising, embers fading. The man points at the camera behind it, shouting. Photorealistic, 3 seconds.
```

## 4 — 20 $ : le billet devient un trio
**Start frame :** plongée sur le comptoir, main qui claque le billet de 20 $.
```
Top-down locked-off shot of a steel kitchen counter. A tattooed hand slaps a green Canadian 20 dollar bill down and pulls away. The bill curls and ignites in a swirling spiral of golden fire and sparks; the flames rise and morph into a combo tray: smash burger, golden fries and a soft drink, appearing exactly where the bill was. Smoke clears. Realistic fire, 4 seconds.
```

## 5 — Rebel sur Uber : onde de choc
**Start frame :** contre-plongée au ras de la plaque, Olivier lève la presse au-dessus de sa tête.
```
Low-angle locked-off shot at griddle level. The man slams the heavy smash press down onto the patty on the griddle; on impact a circular shockwave ripples outward through the air, distorting the background, sparks and grease burst up, the kitchen lights flicker, and the RB logo sign on the wall behind him flares on bright. He looks up into the lens. Photorealistic, slight slow motion on impact, 4 seconds.
```

## 6 — Holy Sh*t : burger géant
**Start frame :** plan large fixe, Olivier pose le pain du dessus sur le burger triple.
```
Wide locked-off shot. The man places the top bun on the triple patty bacon burger and steps back. The burger grows rapidly to giant size, patties, bacon and dripping cheddar scaling up realistically, until the bun touches the ceiling lamps, which swing. The man looks up in shock, tiny next to it, then spreads his arms and shouts at camera. Photorealistic scale, consistent lighting, 4 seconds.
```

## 7 — Le Vicieux : cloche + fumée rouge
**Start frame :** plan rapproché fixe, main d'Olivier sur la cloche en inox.
```
Close locked-off shot. The man lifts the stainless steel cloche in one sharp move; thick crimson smoke explodes out and rolls across the counter, the whole kitchen shifts to deep red light, small lightning arcs crackle inside the smoke. As it clears, the gourmet burger with maple ham and portobello is revealed, glowing with a hot red rim light. Photorealistic, dramatic, 4 seconds.
```

## 8 — Poutine Badass : pierre vs feu
**Start frame :** plan centré symétrique, une poutine dans chaque main.
```
Centered symmetrical locked-off shot. The man holds a plain poutine in his left hand and a loaded poutine in his right. The plain poutine turns gray like stone, cracks and crumbles into dust through his fingers; at the same time the loaded poutine bursts into a small fire on top. He leans in and blows the flame out like a birthday candle, then smirks at camera. Photorealistic, 4 seconds.
```
